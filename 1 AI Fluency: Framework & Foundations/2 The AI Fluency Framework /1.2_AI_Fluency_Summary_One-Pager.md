The AI Fluency
Framework

Four interconnected competencies necessary to ensure our
interactions with AI are effective, efficient, ethical and safe.

The 4D Framework

Key Concepts

Three Modes of AI interaction

 Automation: AI executes specific tasks based on human instruction

 Augmentation: Humans and AI collaborate as thinking partner

 Agency: Humans configure AI to independently perform future tasks

on their behalf

AI Fluency means interacting with AI systems in ways that are
effective, efficient, ethical and safe.

The Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence
Competencies are interconnected collections of skills, knowledge,
insights, values
